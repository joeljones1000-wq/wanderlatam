Questions people ask before they start

Anything else, send it through the trip form and I'll reply within 48 hours.

Do you book anything for me?

No, and that's on purpose. Your plan links every hotel, tour and shuttle, and you book direct with them. You stay in control of your money and your bookings, and there's no agency mark-up.

How long does it take?

I reply to your form within 48 hours. Your draft follows within about 5 business days of your deposit, and the final plan within 2 business days of your changes.

What if I don't like the plan?

Tell me what's wrong and I'll fix it. If the draft just isn't right for you, I'll refund your deposit.

Why a deposit?

I only take on a few trips at a time, and each one gets real research. The deposit reserves your slot and comes off your total. If the draft isn't right for you, it comes back.

I've already booked some things. Can you still help?

Yes. Tell me what's booked in the form and I'll build around it. If most of it's planned already, the itinerary check is the better fit.

Which airport should I fly into?

It depends on the trip. Liberia suits Guanacaste and the northern beaches. San José is usually better for Manuel Antonio and the Caribbean side. Arenal works from either. Flying into one and out of the other can save you a long backtrack, and your plan works that out.

Should I hire a car or use shuttles?

A car gives you freedom, but the required insurance means it costs more than the online price suggests, and you'll want every drive done before dark. Shuttles are easier but less flexible. Your plan says which suits your route and why.

Do I need a 4x4?

Not for every trip. On the main tourist routes a regular car is often fine in the dry season. Some beach areas, rougher back roads and rainy-season routes are where a 4x4 earns its money. Your plan says which you need.

Is this for families?

Very much. Tell me the kids' ages and I'll plan shorter drives, fewer hotel changes, and things they'll actually enjoy.

Can you plan a honeymoon?

Yes. Honeymoon plans move less, slow the pace right down, and are built around the moments worth planning for: the right hot springs, a proper sunset spot, a room worth waking up in.

Does it work for any budget?

Yes. Tell me roughly what you want to spend and I'll plan to it, whether that's simple and good value, a few splurges, or really treating yourselves.

My trip is soon. Can you still help?

Usually, yes. If you're going within 3 weeks, there's a short-notice option that gets you your draft within 2 business days. I check what's still available before recommending it, because the popular places fill up first.

What do I actually receive?

A PDF you can read on your phone or print, with your day-by-day plan, booking links, Google Maps links, the book-ahead list, rain backups, places to eat and packing notes.

What isn't included?

Flights, hotel and tour bookings, airport transfers, car rental, travel insurance and visa applications. I plan it and give you the links. You book and pay the providers directly.

When should I get in touch?

The earlier the better, especially for December to April and holiday weeks like Christmas, New Year and Easter, when the good places and popular tours fill up. Two to six months ahead is ideal, but I can work with less.

What currency will I pay in?

Trip plans are priced in US dollars. If you're elsewhere, the site shows your own currency as a guide, and your bank or card converts it at its rate. Guides are sold through my shop, which charges in British pounds.

Can you plan birding trips?

Yes. Birding plans are built around elevations, lodges and mornings. See birding trips.
