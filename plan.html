An example

What someone might send me

We're a couple, 10 days, first time. We love wildlife, beaches, waterfalls and good food. Medium budget. We'd rather not drive long distances, and we want it relaxed with a bit of adventure.

What I'd suggest

Two bases, not four. La Fortuna for four nights: waterfalls, hanging bridges, hot springs, and one adventure day. Then Manuel Antonio for five: the park on a morning that isn't a Tuesday, and real beach days.

Shared shuttles instead of a rental car, so nobody drives. Monteverde left out on purpose. It's lovely, but for a couple who said no long drives, it adds another long transfer.

Here's what a day in your plan looks like

Day 3: Arenal, the volcano day

Staying: your hotel in La Fortuna, night 3 of 3

  1. 6:30am

    Breakfast early. The volcano is often clearest first thing, before the cloud builds.

  2. 7:30am

    Hanging bridges, before the tour groups arrive. You'll hear the howler monkeys well before you see them.

  3. 10:30am

    La Fortuna Waterfall. A little over 500 steps down and nearly as many back up, so wear good shoes and bring water shoes for the pool. Go before lunch, while the pool is quieter and you've still got the legs for the climb back up.

  4. 12:30pm

    Lunch in town, then a slow afternoon. Every plan has some of these. You'll be glad of it.

  5. 6:00pm

    Hot springs. Rain doesn't matter here. It's even better in it.

If it pours: swap the bridges for a chocolate tour, keep the waterfall if it's safe to swim, and keep the hot springs.

Tomorrow: Monteverde. It's about 3 to 4 hours round Lake Arenal by road, or a jeep, boat and jeep transfer that saves about an hour. Either way, leave after breakfast so you arrive in daylight.
