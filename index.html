Where first trips usually go wrong

None of these are disasters. They just quietly eat days you can't get back.

  • Arenal and Monteverde look like neighbours. By road they're about 3 to 4 hours apart, all the way round the lake. Squeeze both into three days and one of them happens in the car.
  • Manuel Antonio is shut on Tuesdays. Tickets are online only, there's no buying at the gate, and numbers are capped each day. In busy months people turn up and can't get in.
  • The rental car that looked cheap online. Third-party liability insurance is required by law in Costa Rica and can't be taken off, but it's often missing from the price you see when you book. Plenty of people find out at the desk.
  • Driving after dark. It's dark by about six all year round, and a lot of country roads have no lights. A long drive that starts after lunch often finishes in the dark.
  • The wrong coast for your month. The Pacific side's dry season runs roughly December to April. In September and October, the Caribbean side is usually the drier one.
  • The wrong airport. Liberia is about an hour from Tamarindo. San José is usually closer for Manuel Antonio and the Caribbean. Pick the wrong one and your trip can start with an extra half day on the road.
