Privacy

When you send the trip form, I receive your first name, email address and the trip details you type in. I use them to plan your trip and to reply to you. Nothing else.

The form is stored with Wix, the platform this business runs on. Trip plan payments go through Wix's payment system and guide purchases through my store's checkout. I never see your card details.

If you tick the box for Costa Rica tips, I'll occasionally email you. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask me to stop at any time.

I never sell or share your details. To see, change or delete what I hold about you, send a message through the trip form and I'll deal with it.
